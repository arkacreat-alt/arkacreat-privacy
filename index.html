ArKaWorkTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

ArKaWorkTrack is a business workforce management application developed by ArKaCreat. This Privacy Policy explains how information is processed when organisations, administrators and employees use ArKaWorkTrack.

Information We Process

Depending on how an organisation uses ArKaWorkTrack, the application may process information such as:

  • Employee name and employee number
  • Job position and employment information
  • Email address and phone number
  • Workplace assignments
  • Working schedules and contracted working hours
  • Time and attendance information, including clock-in and clock-out records
  • Employee login account information
  • Employee clock codes
  • Information required to administer employee accounts and workforce records

How Information Is Used

Information processed through ArKaWorkTrack is used to provide workforce management functions, including:

  • Managing employees and workplaces
  • Managing employee working schedules
  • Recording employee attendance and working time
  • Authenticating users and protecting accounts
  • Providing administrative workforce management functions
  • Operating, maintaining and securing the ArKaWorkTrack service

QR Code and Scanning Features

ArKaWorkTrack may use QR code scanning as part of employee attendance and workplace functions. Scanning is used to perform the requested application function.

Data Storage and Security

ArKaCreat uses technical and organisational measures designed to protect information processed through ArKaWorkTrack against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or loss.

Information may be processed using cloud infrastructure and service providers necessary to operate the ArKaWorkTrack application and its backend services.

Sharing of Information

ArKaCreat does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers where necessary to operate, host, maintain or secure the ArKaWorkTrack service, or where disclosure is required by law.

Organisation and Administrator Responsibilities

ArKaWorkTrack is designed for use by organisations managing their workforce. Organisations using ArKaWorkTrack are responsible for ensuring that employee information entered into the service is collected and used in accordance with applicable laws and their own policies.

Data Retention and Account Requests

Information is retained as necessary to provide the ArKaWorkTrack service and to meet applicable business, security and legal requirements. Users should contact their organisation or administrator regarding access to, correction of, or deletion of employee information associated with their organisation.

Children

ArKaWorkTrack is a business workforce management application and is not designed or intended for children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when ArKaWorkTrack features, services or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or ArKaWorkTrack privacy practices, please contact ArKaCreat.
